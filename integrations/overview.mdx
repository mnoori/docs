Everything in Patchline is interconnected. **Connectors** extend Aria
into the third-party tools you already use — so a single prompt can
pull from your music data **and** act on your social, project, and
workflow tools.

<TierBadge tier="all" /> &nbsp; Connector actions consume AI credits when Aria calls into the third-party tool.

## What a connector is

A connector is a bridge between Aria and an external service. Aria
authenticates to the service on your behalf (your OAuth, your scopes),
then can do things there from inside a chat:

- *"Post the cover art to my Instagram with this caption."*
- *"Pull recent comments on the YouTube video for the release."*
- *"Add a ClickUp task for tomorrow: 'pitch Lush Sounds curator'."*

## What's available

| Connector | What Aria can do |
|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Read your profile, post media + captions, fetch insights, read comments |
| **YouTube** | Read channel data, fetch video stats, read comments |
| **ClickUp** | Create tasks, attach context from a release, link campaigns to your task board |
| **Notion** | Read pages, append entries, sync release notes |
| **Discord** | Read channel context, post messages |
| **Slack** | Aria-on-Slack is **coming** — today Slack is used for Patchline-side notifications only |

The exact set Aria can call depends on what your workspace has enabled.
If you don't see a connector you want, ask in support and we'll
prioritize.

## How Aria uses them

You don't install a connector explicitly. The first time you ask Aria
to do something that needs one (e.g., *"post this to Instagram"*), Aria
walks you through the connector authorization. Once authorized, future
requests for the same service use the saved authorization automatically.

Authorizations are workspace-scoped, encrypted, and revocable at any
time. Patchline never stores your third-party credentials directly.

## What's NOT a connector

Some things in Patchline look like third-party integrations but are
actually built-in product capabilities, not connector surfaces:

- **Spotify / Apple Music / SoundCloud / YouTube track lookup** — Aria
  reads these via public APIs as part of
  [catalog import](/catalog/import). No OAuth, no setup.
- **Stripe (storefront payouts)** — built into the
  [Music Store](/storefront/overview); you connect Stripe directly
  through the store setup flow.
- **The Patchline product MCP** — Aria itself exposed to your AI tools
  (Claude, Cursor, etc.). See [MCP overview](/for-agents/mcp-overview).

## Examples

### Catalog → social

> *"Post my new release cover art to Instagram with the caption I wrote."*

Aria pulls the cover from your catalog, applies your caption, hands to
the Instagram connector, and confirms the post.

### Release plan → tasks

> *"For each remaining task in my Sleeper release plan, create a ClickUp
> task on my 'Active Releases' list with the due date."*

Aria reads the release plan from your Campaign Planner, iterates each
open task, and creates them in ClickUp via the connector.

### Audience → social reply

> *"Reply to my last 5 Instagram comments — keep the tone warm and
> mention the release coming up."*

Aria pulls the comments, drafts contextual replies, and posts each one
after you approve.

## Pricing

Connector calls consume AI credits like any other Aria action. The
underlying third-party service may have its own usage limits (e.g.,
Instagram API rate limits, YouTube quota) — Aria respects those.
