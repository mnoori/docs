Patchline meters AI actions with **AI credits** — every plan comes with a
monthly credit allowance plus its own capacity limits.

**Plan prices, credit allowances, per-action costs, and limits live on
the [pricing page](https://patchline.ai/pricing).** That's the single
source of truth — we keep the numbers there so they're always current
and never drift out of sync with the docs.
