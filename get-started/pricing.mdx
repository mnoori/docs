## Tier shape

Patchline has five tiers. Each one expands what Aria can act on, how
much you can ship, and which surfaces unlock.

| Tier | Built for |
|---|---|
| **Free** | Try the surface. Limited credits, no music store. |
| **Starter** | Solo artists running their own release flow. |
| **Pro** | DIY labels and managers covering a small roster. |
| **Scale** | Indie labels, A&R teams, multi-artist operations. |
| **Enterprise** | Publishers, large catalogs, custom workflows. |

What changes across tiers:

- **AI credits / month** — the monthly pool that powers every Aria
  action (chat, captioning, image generation, MCP tool calls).
- **Track uploads + catalog imports** — how much you can put in.
- **Connected artists** — how many roster artists you can manage.
- **Music store availability + fee** — included on paid tiers at **0%
  platform fee** (Stripe processing applies separately).
- **Storage** — how much asset storage your workspace gets.
- **Team seats** — multi-user workspace size on Pro+.

For exact numbers, see [patchline.ai/pricing](https://patchline.ai/pricing).

## How AI credits work

A credit is the unit Patchline charges per AI action. Credits reset
each month. Examples of what consumes credits:

- An Aria chat conversation.
- Captioning a new track on import or upload.
- Generating cover art, a promo flyer, or a Spotify Canvas video.
- Calling an MCP tool from your AI editor.
- Acting through a connector (e.g., posting to Instagram).

If you exhaust the pool, Aria pauses new actions and waits for the
next reset (or for you to upgrade).

See [Plans & credits](/account/plans-credits) for per-action math.

## Music storefront

The Music Store is included on **every paid tier** at **0% platform
fee on music sales**. Stripe processing applies separately and goes to
Stripe, not Patchline.

The Free tier does not include the Music Store.

## Billing

- Paid plans are billed monthly via Stripe.
- Upgrade is immediate (prorated for the partial cycle).
- Downgrade takes effect at the next renewal.
- Cancel anytime from Settings → Billing.
