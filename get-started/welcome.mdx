You drop a track, ask a question, or paste a streaming link — and Aria
acts on it. Aria reads your catalog, queries your audience, drafts a
pitch, plans a release, generates artwork, manages your public
surfaces, posts to your connectors.

**Everything in Patchline is interconnected** — one prompt can touch
your whole music ops stack.

Same product, many front doors: the web dashboard, Claude (Desktop or
Claude Code), Cursor, VS Code, Codex, and **Aria on Slack (beta)**.

## What to ask Aria

Aria isn't just a search box. It's a strategist grounded in real
music-business knowledge plus your real data:

- *"What do I do now, and why?"*
- *"What's the best next move for my upcoming release?"*
- *"Which playlist should I pitch first — and why that one?"*
- *"Where is my audience growing right now?"*

Aria reads the music-business knowledge graph plus your account
state, and gives you a grounded recommendation with reasoning — not a
generic answer.

See [Use cases](/aria/prompts) for the pattern library.

## What Patchline is not

Setting expectations matters. Patchline is **not**:

- **An AI music generator.** Patchline does not generate music. You
  make the music; Patchline runs the business around it.
- **A distributor.** Patchline does not ship your music to Spotify,
  Apple, Tidal. You distribute through Distrokid, Tunecore, CD Baby,
  or your label. Patchline picks up the result and runs intelligence
  on it.
- **A DAW.** No multitrack editor.
- **A generic chatbot.** Aria is grounded in your music data and the
  music-business knowledge graph. Ask it about your catalog or your
  next move — you'll get a grounded answer. Ask it about quantum
  physics, it'll politely redirect.

## How Patchline grounds AI answers

Generic AI hallucinates ISRCs, invents stream counts, fabricates
curator names. Patchline's architecture is built to stop that:

- **Your catalog is the source of truth.** Aria reads it directly. If
  data is missing, Aria says "I don't have that" instead of guessing.
- **Streaming and audience signal come from real APIs**, not guesses.
- **AI captions are generated from uploaded audio**, not from the
  title alone.

You can verify any number Aria gives you. That's the design.
