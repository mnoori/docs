A **Project** in Patchline is a single, EP, or album release campaign —
the persistent home for everything that has to happen for that release to
land. Aria generates the campaign for you, end-to-end, against
Patchline's internal **music-business knowledge graph**, grounded in
your artist's stage, your catalog, your audience, and the structural
patterns of releases that have actually worked.

This is the page where Patchline stops being a dashboard and starts
being a release strategist.

<TierBadge tier="all" /> &nbsp; Free tier sees a basic manual project.
Starter and up unlock Aria-generated campaigns and the music-business
knowledge graph.

## What a Project is

When you click **+ Start a project**, Patchline asks four questions:

| Field | Options | Why Aria needs it |
|---|---|---|
| **Artist** | Any artist on your profiles | Anchors the campaign to that artist's audience, catalog history, and stage |
| **Format** | Single &middot; EP &middot; Album | Shapes phase weighting (an album campaign is not a single rollout) |
| **Release path** | Self-releasing &middot; With a label | Decides which tasks you own vs. which a label partner is handling |
| **Project name** | The release title | Threads it through pitches, smart links, fan messaging |

That's the entire intake. From there, Aria does the work.

## What Aria builds

A generated campaign is broken into **six phases**, ordered by how far
from release day each task lands:

| Phase | Window (relative to release) | What lives here |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-production** | day -75 to -46 | Master, artwork, video assets, distribution prep |
| **Planning** | day -45 to -22 | Strategy, pitch list, smart-link build, audience targeting |
| **Pre-release** | day -21 to -8 | Editorial submissions, pre-saves, teaser content, curator outreach |
| **Announcement** | day -7 to -1 | Release-week posts, fan-email send, momentum building |
| **Release** | day 0 to +7 | Release-day execution, storefront, first-week response |
| **Post-release** | day +8 to +42 | TikTok push, follow-up pitches, post-mortem, momentum to next release |

Each phase has a quota of tasks. Aria picks tasks from a curated library
of marketing actions and rewrites them to fit *your* artist and *your*
release — never generic, never copy-paste.

### Aria also chooses the right runway

Tell Aria the release date and the campaign automatically reshapes
around the time you have:

| Runway | Days to release | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Extended** | 84+ | Full pre-production through post-release |
| **Standard** | 43–84 | Balanced, the textbook campaign arc |
| **Short** | 22–42 | Skips deep pre-prod, leans pre-release |
| **Urgent** | 8–21 | Small pre-prod, heavy pre-release + post-release |
| **Release-week** | 0–7 | Almost no pre-prod, all release-day + post-release |
| **Already live** | past release date | Diagnostics + post-release recovery only |

You don't have to know any of this. You set a date; Aria picks the
runway and weights the phases accordingly.

## The music-business knowledge graph (the AI behind it)

This is the part most release tools don't have, and the reason
Patchline's campaigns feel like a strategist wrote them.

Patchline maintains an internal **music-business knowledge graph** — a
clean-room, Patchline-native model of how the music business actually
moves: which tactics work for which artist stage, which channels matter
for which genre, when editorial windows open, what curators actually
respond to, how releases of different shapes have rolled out. The graph
is continuously rebuilt from sanitized industry signal — never copied,
always rewritten into Patchline's own taxonomy of **concepts &middot;
tactics &middot; decision rules &middot; runway buckets &middot;
channels**.

When Aria builds your campaign, it runs a structured query against the
graph for *your specific situation*:

- Your artist's stage (emerging, developing, established, etc. — derived
  from your monthly listeners and growth trajectory).
- Your release format (single vs. EP vs. album shapes very different
  campaigns).
- Your goals (launching, growing existing traction, or sustaining a
  catalog cycle).
- Your channels (which connectors and storefronts you've wired up).
- Your runway (how long you have to ship).

The graph returns sanitized **learning units** — abstract music-business
concepts, neutral practical tactics, decision rules for when each
applies, and the actual task candidates that implement them. Aria fuses
those with your real data (the track's sonic profile, your roster
audience, your last release's performance) to produce a campaign that is
both grounded in industry reality and specific to your release.

<Note>
  The knowledge graph is sanitized by design. It contains no source
  names, no people, no competitor references, no scraped URLs — only the
  reasoning patterns. That's why Patchline can give you strategist-level
  guidance without leaking anyone's secret sauce, and without exposing
  you to outdated or risky tactics from unverified blogs.
</Note>

## What's in a generated task

Every task in your campaign is more than a checkbox. It carries:

- **Phase &middot; due date** — where it lives in the timeline.
- **Channel** — streaming, social, community, playlist-pitching,
  publicity, ads, creative, operations, analytics.
- **Why it's here** — the decision rule from the knowledge graph that
  picked it.
- **What Aria can do for you** — many tasks are one-click: *"draft this
  pitch,"* *"assemble this DM list,"* *"build this smart link."*
- **Patchline surface link** — if the task wires into a Patchline
  feature (a Smart Link, Pitch Link, Drop Link, Media Kit, Store Link,
  Briefing Link), it's deep-linked so you can act in one click.

  <Step title="Pick the artist & track">

    <AriaPromptCard prompt="Start a project for my new single 'Sleeper' — target date in 6 weeks, self-releasing." />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your release date & goal">

    also offers promo tiers — **essentials** (organic-first),

  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Aria queries the music-business knowledge graph, fuses it with your

    Edit any task that doesn't fit. Click **Save** to persist the

    up today?"* and Aria walks the next tasks. As you ship, mark them
    off — Aria adjusts downstream timing based on what actually
    happened.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When to use it

- **You've finished a track and don't know where to start.** Open
  Projects and ask Aria. You'll have a draft campaign in under a minute.
- **You're managing multiple artists with overlapping schedules.**
  Projects let you track every campaign independently without losing
  state — the underlying storage is concurrent-write-safe, so you can
  have a project open across tabs and devices without overwriting
  yourself.
- **Your last release flopped and you want a smarter rollout.** Aria
  pulls the comparable releases on your roster and the comparable
  patterns from the knowledge graph — *"the dreampop releases that
  worked all had X in week -4; yours didn't, so it's in this campaign."*
- **You're working with a label and need to show what you own.**
  Pick the *with a label* path — Aria filters to artist/team-owned
  tasks and leaves the label-side moves out.

## What a campaign looks like

A typical 8-week campaign for a dreampop single (Mira's "Sleeper"):

```text
🎵  Sleeper — Mira — Single, self-releasing
    Target release: 2026-07-15 (in 8 weeks — standard runway)
    Sonic positioning: dreampop-adjacent, slow, melancholy, soft acoustic

━ PRE-PRODUCTION (day -75 to -46) ─────────────────────────────
  □ Finalize master + alt-mix references
  □ Lock cover art + canvas video
  □ Confirm distribution timeline
  □ Brief any collaborators on release-week deliverables

━ PLANNING (day -45 to -22) ───────────────────────────────────
  □ Build Patchline Smart Link (pre-save + storefront wired in)
  □ Assemble DM list: 11 dreampop curators (Aria pulled from your
    similar-artist data — 8 added Tessa's "Long Way")
  □ Draft Spotify editorial pitch — submission window opens day -28

━ PRE-RELEASE (day -21 to -8) ─────────────────────────────────
  □ Submit Spotify editorial pitch via S4A
  □ Submit Apple Music for Artists editorial
  □ Pre-save go-live
  □ Schedule fan email (Mira list: 1,247 subscribers)
  □ Personal DMs to the 8 priority curators
  □ Teaser content drop (Patchline media kit)

━ ANNOUNCEMENT (day -7 to -1) ─────────────────────────────────
  □ Release-week social posts (3 platforms — drafts in Aria)
  □ Fan-email final send
  □ Storefront banner swap

━ RELEASE (day 0 to +7) ───────────────────────────────────────
  □ Release-day post (Aria has the drafts)
  □ TikTok teaser drop
  □ First-week DM follow-ups to curators who added
  □ Storefront launch

━ POST-RELEASE (day +8 to +42) ────────────────────────────────
  □ TikTok ad test — $50, dreampop cluster (your last 3 releases
    that did this beat forecast by 2.1×)
  □ Follow-up pitch to curators who didn't add (Aria drafts)
  □ Week 2 performance review
  □ Post-mortem: streams vs forecast
  □ Hand momentum to next release
```

Each item is an actionable, dated task. Aria can execute many of them
itself — draft the pitch, assemble the DM list, schedule the smart
link, queue the post — when you ask.

## How it works (the system, not the marketing)

1. The Projects UI calls Aria with the intake (artist, format,
   distribution path, title, date, goal, promo tier).
2. Aria builds a **graph query** with your situation: artist stage,
   format, channels, runway bucket, goal.
3. The query hits Patchline's **music-business knowledge graph** — a
   sanitized, Patchline-native model of music-industry reasoning
   patterns. The graph returns the learning units that apply.
4. Aria fuses graph context with **your data**: the track's sonic
   profile, your roster's audience, your historical release
   performance, your connector inventory.
5. The campaign generator selects 30–50 tasks across the six phases,
   rewrites each one for *your* release, assigns a date in the right
   phase window, and links the relevant Patchline surface (smart link,
   pitch link, media kit, etc.).
6. The campaign is persisted as a project. Edits are concurrent-write
   safe — the underlying storage uses optimistic-concurrency guards so
   two tabs editing the same project don't overwrite each other.
7. As you ship tasks, Aria can re-plan downstream — *"you missed the
   editorial window, here's the recovery path."*

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where does the music-business knowledge graph come from?">
    Patchline builds it. The graph is a sanitized, Patchline-native
    model — no source names, no scraped content, no competitor
    references. Every entry is a clean-room rewrite into concepts,
    tactics, decision rules, and Patchline-native task candidates. The
    graph is versioned and continuously rebuilt as industry signal
    evolves.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the campaign after Aria generates it?">
    Yes. Every task is editable — rename, change date, change owner,
    add description, mark complete. You can also ask Aria to revise —
    *"move pre-release stuff earlier,"* *"drop the TikTok ad,"* *"add
    a storefront launch task in release week."* The campaign re-shapes
    around your changes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if my release is in 3 weeks, not 8?">
    The knowledge graph has runway-aware quotas. A 3-week release lands
    in the **urgent** bucket — Aria builds a small pre-prod, a focused
    pre-release, and weights heavily on post-release recovery. You give
    it the date, it picks the right shape.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can multiple people work on the same project?">
    Yes — multi-user workspaces are available on higher tiers. The
    project storage uses optimistic-concurrency guards so two
    teammates editing the same project don't overwrite each other.
    See [pricing](/get-started/pricing) for workspace sizes.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does Aria actually submit to Spotify editorial?">
    No. Spotify for Artists doesn't have a public submission API. Aria
    drafts the pitch, reminds you when the window opens, and
    deep-links you to S4A; you submit there. Same pattern for Apple
    Music for Artists.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I miss a deadline?">
    Ask Aria *"what's overdue"* — it flags the misses and suggests
    recovery actions from the graph. If you miss an editorial window,
    Aria proposes alternative paths (direct DMs, curator marketplaces,
    earned-media angles) ranked by what works for your stage.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the graph use my data?">
    Your data grounds *your* campaign — your artist stage, your
    catalog, your audience, your release history. It does not get
    written back into the public graph. The graph is built from
    sanitized industry signal, not from any individual artist's
    private data.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What's the difference between a Project and a release?">
    A Project is the campaign — the planning, the tasks, the
    timeline, the assets. A release is what happens on release day.
    One Project produces one release (single, EP, or album). Your
    Project lives on past release day; it holds the post-release
    momentum-capture phase.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
