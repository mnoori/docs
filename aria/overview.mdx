the same way you talk to a manager, A&R, or label-ops person — and Aria
takes action: reads your catalog, queries streaming data, drafts pitches,
plans releases, updates project anchors, generates artwork briefs.

## When to use Aria

Use Aria for the work that lives between tools — the strategy and
synthesis that usually means hopping between Spotify for Artists, your
DAW, a label spreadsheet, and a notes app.

- **You just finished a track and don't know what to do next.** Paste the
  file or the link. Aria gives you a release plan grounded in your
  catalog, your audience, and what's actually working for artists like you
  right now.
- **A curator asked for a pitch and you have an hour.** "Pitch this track
  to Mint Friday curators" returns a draft you can edit and send.
- **You want to know if you have momentum.** "Summarize my streaming for
  the last month" pulls the real numbers, not vibes.
- **You're managing 5 artists and an upcoming release schedule.** "What
  do I owe on Mira's release this week?" reads the project anchor and
  returns the shortlist.

## One agent, many disciplines

Aria is built as an **orchestrator** — not a single LLM with a long
system prompt, but a coordination layer that holds your full context
and routes work across many specialized capabilities under the hood.

What that means in practice:

- **Aria has access to your entire dataset.** Profiles, catalog,
  releases, playlists, visuals, music store, launchpad, audience,
  connectors. One conversation can pull from all of them.
- **Aria coordinates many specialist capabilities.** Artist research,
  catalog captioning, playlist matching, release strategy, pitch
  drafting, audience reads, visual generation, storefront ops,
  connector actions. You see one assistant. Behind it, dozens of
  capabilities are being chosen, sequenced, and combined to answer
  your prompt.
- **Aria reads the music-business knowledge graph.** Aria isn't just
  reasoning over your data — it's also reasoning over a deep,
  continuously updated model of the music business: what works for
  which artist tier, which playlists are moving, which audiences are
  growing where. That's what makes "what do I do now, and why?" a
  real question with a real answer.

This is why we built Aria as **one agent to rule them all**, instead
of shipping ten separate agents you'd have to switch between. The
work in the music business doesn't live in silos — your audience is
your release strategy is your storefront. Your assistant shouldn't
either.

## Grounded by design

Every Aria answer is grounded in real data — your account state plus
the music-business knowledge graph. Aria does not invent ISRCs,
fabricate stream counts, or guess curator names. If a piece of data
isn't available, Aria says so and offers to fetch it.

You can verify any number Aria gives you. That's the design.

## Privacy and control

- Your data stays scoped to your workspace.
- Patchline does not use your catalog, audience data, or chat
  history to train models. (Hard rule.)
- Connector authorizations are revocable from your account at any
  time.
- Public surfaces you create (smart links, drop links, pitch links)
  expose only what you explicitly publish — your raw catalog and
  internal data are not in those public pages.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does Aria train on my data?">
    No. Patchline does not use your catalog, audience data, or chat
    history to train models. Your data is used to ground Aria's answers
    in the current conversation only.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What model does Aria use?">
    Aria runs on hosted frontier AI models combined with Patchline-built
    tooling. Exact model versions and providers may change as quality,
    latency, and safety improve. Patchline does not use your catalog,
    statements, or chat history to train models.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can Aria send emails or post to social media?">
    Aria can act through your [connectors](/integrations/overview) —
    Instagram, ClickUp, Notion, and more. For surfaces not yet available
    as connectors, Aria drafts the copy and you publish it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why did Aria refuse to answer my question?">
    Aria is grounded in your data. If you ask about a track that isn't in
    your catalog or an artist you haven't added to your roster, Aria will
    say so and offer to import. This is by design — better a clear "I
    don't have that" than a confident-sounding wrong answer.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there an API I can call directly?">
    Yes — for AI agents. Patchline's product MCP server at
    [`www.patchline.ai/api/mcp/v1`](/for-agents/mcp-overview) exposes the
    public tool surface. A direct REST API is Enterprise-only today.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if Aria gets it wrong?">
    Send feedback via the thumbs-down on any chat message. The signal
    feeds product improvement. We
    review every thumbs-down weekly.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
